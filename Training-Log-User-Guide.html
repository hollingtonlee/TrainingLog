Training Log

User Guide

A quick reference for everything the app can do.

Logging a Session

Tap + Add workout and fill in:

  • Name — e.g. "Chest Press" or "Indoor Bike"
  • Category — Strength, Cardio, Mobility, or Other
  • Date, Duration, and Body weight (optional)
  • Sets / Reps
  • Weight/Distance — a number plus a unit dropdown (lbs, kg, miles, meters, or yds). Use it for weight lifted or distance covered — e.g. 2100 + meters for rowing.
  • Notes

Tap 📋 Copy from previous inside the form to pre-fill everything from something you've logged before, then just adjust the date.

Editing or Deleting a Session

Tap any entry in your log to open it for editing. A red Delete entry button sits at the bottom of that screen.

To delete an entire day at once, tap the 🗑 icon next to that day's heading (TODAY, YESTERDAY, etc.). You'll get a confirmation first — this can't be undone.

Weight Session Checklist 🏋️

For gym days with many exercises, tap the 🏋️ icon in the header instead of logging each one through the form.

  • Check a box → instantly logs that exercise to today with its saved weight/sets/reps.
  • Uncheck → removes that logged entry.
  • Reopen later the same day → already-checked items stay checked, so you can pick up mid-session.
  • Tap ✎ next to any exercise to edit its weight, sets, reps, or remove it from the list.
  • + Add exercise adds a new move to your checklist.

This list is a reusable template, separate from your actual log — checking it off doesn't use it up.

Reading Your Log

  • Days with more than one session get a vertical line connecting them, so you can see at a glance you did several things that day.
  • A day with 2+ exercises logged via the weights checklist collapses into one row: "🏋️ Weight session · N exercises." Tap it to expand or collapse.
  • Tap Newest first / Oldest first to flip the sort order.
  • The stats bar shows sessions in the last 7 days, hours this month, and your current day streak.
  • Tap 📊 for a fuller summary broken down by period.

Profiles

Tap the Me ▾ chip under the title to switch between or create separate training logs — handy if more than one person shares the app.

Backup & Restore

Tap Backup to:

  • Export your full log as a JSON file — do this regularly, especially before switching devices.
  • Restore from backup by choosing a previously exported JSON file. New entries are added; anything you already have is skipped, so it's safe to re-import.

If you haven't backed up in a while (or ever), the app will pop the backup screen open automatically when you launch it, as a friendly reminder.

Appearance

Tap 🎨 to change the app's color theme.
